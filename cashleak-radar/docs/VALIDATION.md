# 72-hour validation plan

1. Interview 15 small-business owners/accountants about overdue payments and reconciliation.
2. Run their sanitized historical exports through the MVP manually.
3. Measure how many true anomalies are found and the value of unresolved money.
4. Ask whether they would pay monthly for the report and recovery workflow.
5. Do not build WhatsApp/banking integrations until repeated customer demand is confirmed.
